How to play Risk Seven

Be first to 200 points. Each hand you collect number cards with different values. Draw the same number twice and that hand scores nothing. Collect seven different numbers in one hand and the hand ends: you take your total plus 15 extra points.

Your choice

Each hand starts with one card each. Then, in order, you either hit (take one more card) or stay (keep this hand’s points). The hand ends when nobody is still hitting, or when someone reaches seven different numbers.

What’s in the pile

Number cards from 0 to 12. There is one 0, one 1, two 2s, and so on up to twelve 12s. Mixed in: three Lock cards, three Triple cards, three Spare cards, and one each of +2, +4, +6, +8, +10, and ×2.

How points add up

Add the numbers in your line. If you have ×2, double that sum. Then add any + cards. Then add 15 if you made seven. A busted hand is zero. If two or more people cross 200 in the same hand and share the top total, keep playing hands until one person leads.

Action cards

  • Lock — hand it to anyone still in (including you). They bank what they have and sit out the rest of the hand.
  • Triple — that person must take the next three cards, one by one. Stop early on a bust or a seven. A Spare found in those three is handed out at once. A Lock or Triple found in those three is set aside and handed out only after the three cards are finished (and only if that person did not bust or hit seven).
  • Spare — keep it if you do not already have one. The next matching number is thrown away instead of busting you. Only one Spare at a time; a second copy goes to someone else who is still in.

Whoever draws Lock or Triple chooses the recipient. If you draw Lock and you are the only person still in, it banks you and the hand ends. If you draw Triple and you are the only person still in, it has to land on you.

Keys: H hit, S stay, 1–7 pick someone. The next hand starts on its own after the scoreboard.
